WHO WE ARE

Rkoshi Packers & Movers Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted name in the relocation industry, serving customers across Bihar and Jharkhand for over a decade. What started as a small local moving service has grown into a comprehensive logistics solution provider, helping thousands of families and businesses relocate with confidence.


We are more than just a moving company—we are your partners in transition. Our commitment to quality service, customer satisfaction, and ethical business practices has earned us the trust of countless clients who rely on us for their most important moves.



Our Experience & Expertise

With over 10 years of industry experience, we have developed deep expertise in all aspects of packing and moving. Our team has successfully completed over 15,000 relocations, ranging from small household moves to large corporate office shifts.

Our experience spans diverse scenarios—from transporting delicate antiques and expensive electronics to relocating entire manufacturing setups. This breadth of experience enables us to handle any moving challenge with efficiency and care.
